Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How quickly can you start our project? +
We typically provide a detailed risk assessment and fixed-cost proposal within 3-5 business days. Installation can begin as soon as 1-2 weeks after approval, depending on project size and equipment availability. For urgent go-live deadlines, we can often expedite timelines.
Do you work evenings and weekends? +
Yes. We understand healthcare environments operate 24/7. Most of our installations are performed during evenings, weekends, or off-peak hours to minimize disruption to patient care and clinical workflows. This is included in our standard service—not an upcharge.
What if we're not sure which mounting solution we need? +
That's what we're here for. We provide workflow analysis and install evaluation units so your staff can test different options before you commit. We'll guide you to the best solution based on your clinical workflows, space constraints, and budget—not just what's easiest to install.
Can you maintain equipment we didn't purchase through you? +
Absolutely. We service most makes and models of IT mounting hardware, regardless of where you purchased it. Our lifecycle management services help you maximize ROI on existing investments through preventative maintenance and timely repairs.
How do you handle infection control requirements? +
Infection control is built into everything we do. All technicians are trained on healthcare infection prevention protocols. We eliminate floor cables, conceal exposed hardware, create cleanable surfaces, and stow equipment off the floor. We coordinate with your infection control team throughout the project.
What's included in your fixed-cost proposals? +
Everything: labor, materials, hardware procurement, project coordination, daily progress reporting, staff training, and warranty. The only time we issue change orders is if you change the project scope. No hidden fees, no surprise charges.
Do your installers have healthcare credentials? +
Yes. All our installation technicians are factory-trained, background-checked, and credentialed to work in healthcare environments. They understand patient privacy, clinical workflows, and how to work in sensitive areas without disrupting care.
